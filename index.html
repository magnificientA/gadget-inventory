Gadget Inventory

Stock, sales and IMEI tracking for phone and gadget shops, installed on your own Windows computer.

  • Track phones one by one by IMEI, and accessories by quantity.
  • Record sales, payments, customer balances, returns and faults.
  • Daily summaries, Excel export, and backups you control.
  • Works without internet. Your records stay on your own computer.

Google Drive backups (optional)

A business owner can choose to connect their own Google Drive so the app saves a daily backup copy of that business's records there. The app asks only for permission to create and manage the backup files it makes itself. It can't see any other files in the owner's Drive. Read the privacy policy.

Who makes it

Gadget Inventory is made by Accord Gadgets for independent businesses. It is installed and run by each business on its own computer; there is no central server that holds business records.
