Privacy Policy

Gadget Inventory · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Gadget Inventory is a stock and sales app that each business installs and runs on its own Windows computer. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: your business records stay on your own computer, and the app's maker does not receive, collect or see them.

Information the app stores

The app stores the information you enter: your business name, logins for you and your staff (passwords are stored only in scrambled, hashed form), stock and IMEI numbers, suppliers, customers and their phone numbers, sales, payments, expenses, returns, fault notes and photos, your logo, and daily summaries. All of this is kept in the app's data folder on the computer where it is installed (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Gadget Inventory).

The app has no central server, no advertising and no analytics. It does not send your records to Accord Gadgets or anyone else. Separate installations of the app do not share or sync records with each other.

Optional Google Drive backups

If, and only if, a business owner chooses Connect Google Drive, the app uses Google's sign-in to ask for permission to create and manage the files it makes itself in that owner's own Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). The app cannot see, open or change any other files in your Drive.

  • What is uploaded: a backup copy of that one business's records, settings, logo and fault photos, saved to a folder in the owner's own Drive once a day while the app is running. The newest 30 daily copies are kept; older copies the app made are deleted.
  • Who can see it: the backups are in the owner's own Google account, under that owner's control. They are not sent to Accord Gadgets.
  • Your Google sign-in: the app never sees your Google password. Google gives the app an access key, which is stored encrypted on the business's own computer and used only to make, list, download and remove its backup files.
  • Stopping: owners can press Disconnect in the app at any time, which deletes the stored access key and tells Google to cancel it. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from Google is used only to provide the backup feature. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not transferred to anyone else.

Keeping records safe

Logins are protected with hashed passwords, the app accepts connections only from the computer it runs on, and staff accounts see only what the owner allows. Each business is responsible for the security of its own computer and for keeping its own backups.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling the app does not delete records. To delete them completely, delete the app's data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Gadget Inventory) and any backup files you saved, including those in your Google Drive.
